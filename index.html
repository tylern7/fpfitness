Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need experience to start?

Not at all. No experience is necessary. Every session is coached and scaled to your level, so complete beginners train right alongside seasoned athletes and both get exactly what they need.

What happens on my first visit?

You’ll meet your coach, walk through the facility, and talk about your goals before you train. We’ll show you the movements, set your starting point, and make sure you feel comfortable from the first minute.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, clean indoor training shoes, and a water bottle. That’s it — we have all the equipment you need.

Can you help me lose weight?

Many members come to us with that goal. We pair small-group training with nutrition coaching and accountability check-ins, and build a sustainable plan around your lifestyle instead of a crash diet. Everyone’s results are different, and we’ll set realistic goals together at your intro.

How much does it cost?

Memberships range from $30 to $500 per month depending on the program. Request our current pricing, or call us at (289) 207-7709 and we’ll help you find the right fit.

Where are you located and is there parking?

We’re at 3325 Harvester Road, Unit 14 in Burlington, Ontario, serving Burlington, Oakville, and Hamilton. There’s free on-site parking right outside the unit.

Do you offer drop-in sessions?

We do. Drop-ins are $35 per class. Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete your waiver and get set up with the coach before the session starts. Request a drop-in.
